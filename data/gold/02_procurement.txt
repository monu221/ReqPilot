STORY 1: As a department staff member, I want to submit a purchase request
through an online form, so that requests are complete and not lost in email.
AC1: Given I am logged in, when I submit a request without a budget code,
then it is not submitted and the missing budget code is shown.
AC2: Given I am submitting a request, when no quote is attached, then it is
not submitted and I am asked to attach at least one quote.
AC3: Given all required fields and a quote are provided, when I submit,
then the request is saved with a reference number.

STORY 2: As a finance controller, I want each request routed to the right
approvers based on its amount, so that the approval rules are applied
consistently.
AC1: Given a request under 5,000 EUR, when it is submitted, then only the
department head is asked to approve.
AC2: Given a request between 5,000 and 50,000 EUR, when it is submitted,
then the department head and finance are asked to approve.
AC3: Given a request over 50,000 EUR, when it is submitted, then the
department head, finance and the managing director are asked to approve.

STORY 3: As a requester, I want to see the status of my request at any
time, so that I know where it is in the approval process.
AC1: Given I submitted a request, when I open it, then I see its current
status and which approver it is waiting for.

STORY 4: As an approver, I want to receive a reminder when a request is
waiting for me, so that requests are not delayed.
AC1: Given a request has waited for my approval longer than the reminder
period, when the period ends, then I receive a reminder.

STORY 5: As an approver, I want to reject a request with a reason, so that
the requester knows what to change.
AC1: Given I reject a request, when I do not enter a reason, then the
rejection is not saved.
AC2: Given I reject a request with a reason, when I save, then the
requester is notified with the reason.

STORY 6: As a finance controller, I want an audit trail of all approval
actions, so that I can see who approved what and when.
AC1: Given any approval or rejection, when it is saved, then the user, the
action, the date and the time are recorded and cannot be changed.

STORY 7: As a procurement manager, I want approved requests exported to the
ERP system, so that orders do not have to be entered twice.
AC1: Given a request is fully approved, when the export runs, then the
request appears in the ERP system.
