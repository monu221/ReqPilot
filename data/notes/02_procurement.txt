Workshop: purchase request approvals, 19 Nov
Organisation: Northfield Clinic (invented)
Attendees: procurement manager (Lena), finance controller (Marc),
department head of radiology (Dr. Okafor), IT (Tom)

- departments send purchase requests by email with an Excel form today
- Lena: requests are incomplete, no budget code, no quote attached
- want an online request form with required budget code and at least one quote
- Marc: approval depends on amount
  - under 5,000 EUR: department head only
  - 5,000 to 50,000 EUR: department head and finance
  - over 50,000 EUR: also the managing director
- Dr. Okafor: approvals take weeks, nobody knows where a request is
- requester should see the status at any time
- reminder if an approver does not act, Marc suggested after a few days
- approver must be able to reject with a reason
- Lena wants to approve urgent requests faster, "urgent" not defined
- export of approved requests to the ERP system, Tom to check if possible
- audit trail needed, Marc: "who approved what and when"
