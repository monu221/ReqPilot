Workshop: lab results to national health record, 24 Nov
Organisation: Northfield Clinic (invented), national exchange hub "NHX" (invented)
Attendees: lab manager (Priya), integration lead (Jonas), data protection
officer (Mrs. Weber), clinician representative (Dr. Lin)

- lab results stay in the hospital system today, other providers cannot see them
- NHX requires results sent as FHIR resources, Jonas has the spec
- send results automatically once the lab validates them
- Priya: corrected results must replace the earlier version, not duplicate it
- Dr. Lin: critical results must still be phoned to the doctor, as today
- Mrs. Weber: send only if the patient has consented, consent stored in the HIS
- every message must be authenticated, NHX uses tokens
- if NHX is down, messages should not be lost, retry later
- Jonas: NHX can reject a message, someone must see and fix the rejections
- volume roughly 3,000 results per day, peak in the morning
- should be "near real time", nobody defined a number
- go-live wanted before the national deadline, date not confirmed
